Dolmenwood: Fantasy RPG built around the acclaimed Old-School Essentials rules

Dolmenwood is a fantasy RPG built around the acclaimed Old-School Essentials rules, presenting a familiar game with a lavishly detailed world inspired by the fairy tales and eerie folklore of the British Isles. Streamlined rules and helpful introductory materials guide new players, while veteran players experience a fresh sense of the unknown, with new character options, magic, monsters, and treasures.
