What is w2rt6byl5nf1opmc-a.com?

I’m normal. This means I have a backlink monitor for this website. By pure chance, I was showcasing this to a friend when, suddenly, I got a new backlink:

I tap in,1 forgetting that 90% of backlinks I get are spam, and maybe this one could log my IP or even contain the 3-day2 exploit chain of doom. I’m greeted with this:

I sent an early draft of this article to another friend, who happens to be Vietnamese, and he told me there’s five casino spam sites ahead of me, which I already knew because I’m Faviconese. I have the most popular personal website on .cab,3 but that’s probably because I have the only personal website on .cab.

I try some other TLDs. Rebane has the #1 .horse, synth.download is the 11th .download, whatever.

Redacted? I hover over it and discover it goes to windows.net.siterate.org.4 Figuring out the pattern of redactions is not the topic of this article.

I try .com.

What the fuck????

I visited this domain (two days after it was registered, more on that in a second) and Firefox gave me the , dnsNotFound. To this day it doesn’t resolve.

I do a web search of “w2rt6byl5nf1opmc-a.com” and I see references to the Majestic Million, a name I haven’t heard in ages. Sure enough, that’s what siterate.org uses:

The Majestic Million ranks domains based on the number of different subnets linking to them.5 This domain is the fastest growing in the top 100.

Another, maybe unrelated anomaly I saw is ailawandorder.com (registered in 2023) at rank 80, whose “About Us” page says its CEO is serving as a Board Member at Majestic. This made me check rank 800 in case there was a pattern, and no, there isn’t, that’s nsw.gov.au.

Before I got rate limited from Majestic, I opened Site Explorer, and it told me w2rt6byl5nf1opmc-a.com has 241,085,378 nofollow backlinks and 6 follow backlinks split across 5,163,708 referring domains. Checking Ahrefs, I see just 53 backlinks; they read like the ones I’ve been getting since The Article fell off.

All the backlinks on Majestic are “fresh”, which means in the last 120 days. In fact, the domain was bought (not by me 💔) for the first time (according to Certificate Transparency) on November 11, after most of the backlinks. I found an AI-generated PR from 2 weeks ago referencing the domain, and Tranco has data from October 5.

Majestic has indexed 1,267 pages on this domain and crawled 27. The site isn’t on the Wayback Machine so I can’t collect its pages.

In the Anchor Text table, the link count adds up to nine. There isn’t an “other” section. Are the remaining 240 million links images? No, they aren’t, it says the links are 100% TextLinks.

Then I downloaded Majestic Million myself and searched for the domain. There were six subdomains.

For comparison, jack.cab, which you’ve clearly heard of, is rank 804,163. What the fuck is going on?

By the way, w2rt6byl5nf1opmc-a.com is at rank 38 on Tranco, which was designed to protect against this but still uses Majestic data.

Theories

I could probably figure out what’s happening here in 30 seconds by buying a Majestic subscription but I’m lazy.

  • Copyright trap: maybe it’s fake, injected into the list by Majestic so they can discover who’s using their list. Could w2rt6byl5nf1opmc-a.com and ailawandorder.com be in the top 100 for this reason? Probably not, because if they cared, they’d relicense the list away from CC-BY. Also, the domains are ranking higher in Majestic over time instead of staying static.
  • Majestic got hacked, and the hackers for some reason decided to tamper with the DB like this. No. Are you stupid, are you dumb, are you Dario Amodei, are you all three‽
  • Some loophole/gaming the rankings: Maybe “subnets” is counted in a stupid way for IPv6. Maybe the millions of domains are ip6.arpa. Majestic counts Class C subnets.
  • A bunch of hacked websites linking to the domain: Seems unlikely, considering I’m unable to find a single backlink in the wild that wouldn’t be there for say, my website, and Ahrefs only has 53 backlinks for this domain. They could be cloaking.
