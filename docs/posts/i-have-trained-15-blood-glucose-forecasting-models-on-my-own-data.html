Show HN: I have trained 15 blood glucose forecasting models on my own data

Note

Most of the code in this repo was written by AI. If you don't like that, feel free to leave.

This repo is a zoo. Here I train & test many blood glucose forecasting models (including my own) on both my own data and metabonet (public).

My model is a small transformer that forecasts blood glucose 2 hours ahead. I've made two variants: D16 DILATE and D16 MSE which use DILATE and MSE loss for training, respectively. D16 refers to the width of the model (16). They are both trained on synthetic traces, and then a LoRA adapter is added for light fine-tuning, but I also test them without the adapter.

I have type 1 diabetes and I wanna know if I can train a BG forecasting model to help me with my daily management. To this end I've made some auxiliary tools:

  • T1DMSIM creates synthetic CGM traces since UVA/Padova was too slow on my laptop.
  • T1DMDROID runs the models on my phone, also has some neat functionality like reading BG from multiple CGMs at once.

For evaluation, I use the recommendations by Jacobs et al., IEEE Reviews in Biomedical Engineering, 2024 [1]. It's a nice paper that you should probably read.

All models suck. XGBoost is the best one but my own model comes close. More details below:

  1. On my data, D16 DILATE + LoRA comes 3rd of 26 (22.8), behind XGBoost (22.2) and LightGBM (22.4). Wilcoxon puts it level with both at 60 and 90 min, behind them at 30 and 120.
  2. On MetaboNet, D16 DILATE + LoRA comes 9th (34.6) but has the lowest RMSE of all models at 90 min. XGBoost leads at 30, 60 and 120.
  3. At 60 min a 3771 parameter LoRA adapter helps both models on both datasets: 26.1 to 22.8 and 27.1 to 23.5 on my data, 36.6 to 34.6 and 36.7 to 35.1 on MetaboNet. At 120 min on MetaboNet it makes D16 DILATE worse (48.0 to 50.4).
  4. On MetaboNet, LoRA also fixes the bands. The 90% band holds the truth 75-78% of the time at 60 min with sim only and 81-85% with LoRA. On my data both are 83-85%.
  5. T1DMAI + LoRA reacts earliest on my data: 40-50 min time gain at 60 min, against 30 for XGBoost.
  6. It also catches the most lows on my data: 40-47% at 60 min, against 29% for XGBoost. On MetaboNet every low-RMSE model misses nearly all lows (XGBoost 0%, T1DMAI 6-8%), while linear extrapolation catches 58%.
  7. LightGBM matches XGBoost within 0.2 mg/dL everywhere. An LSTM with 15 hours of history comes 3rd on MetaboNet (33.6 at 60 min), against 35.9 for the 2 hour one.
  8. Zero-shot Chronos-2 and TimesFM 2.5 beat zero-shot Chronos-Bolt by 1.4-2.7 mg/dL at 60 min on both datasets, but trail fine-tuned Chronos-Bolt.

Numbers in brackets point to References. Everything except the baselines and the foundation models was trained from scratch on each dataset's training split. T1DMAI comes in two versions: sim only is the checkpoint in models/, and sim + LoRA adds a frozen-base rank 1 adapter [23] (3771 parameters) trained on the real training split.

T1DMAI details
Architecture encoder-only transformer, 30 min patches, one attention head, RoPE [26], SwiGLU
Size 16 layers of width 16, 31k parameters
Loss 7 quantile pinball loss plus a median term (DILATE [25] on soft-DTW [24], or MSE)
Inputs CGM in a risk-scaled space, carb absorption curve, insulin action curve, mask bit
Output 7 quantiles (5% to 95%) for every 5 minute step of the next 2 hours
History up to 7 days
Simulator training 1M simulated people, 7 days each, 10000 steps of batch 512
Runtime ExecuTorch or LiteRT, on the phone in T1DMDROID

More in docs/USAGE.md and ARCHITECTURE.md.
