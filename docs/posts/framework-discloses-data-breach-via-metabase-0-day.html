Quoting the data breach email:

Quoting the Privacy Policy:

“We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer to outside parties your personally identifiable information. This does not include trusted third parties who assist us in operating our site, conducting our business, or servicing you, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.”

Put together, it means you shared personally identifiable information that was not needed to a third party (here Metabase), and it was by design because you included it in the Privacy Policy (Metabase is in “conducting your business” as a BI platform).

You don’t need my full name, email address or complete billing address in Metabase. Why send my private data there in the first place ? I don’t care if they “agree to keep this information confidential”, good will doesn’t prevent data breaches and this was bound to happen sooner or later.

Checking the data shared with Metabase is not enough. What needs to be done is a full data privacy audit to check if your customers’ right to data privacy are respected (including in your project management framework). In other words, are you sending data to platforms that don’t need it ? When handling implementation projects, do you have a data analysis to cover these risks ?

I don’t want to receive the same email for another of your partners in 6 months. Respecting your customers doesn’t stop at the hardware level. It also means ensuring that you’re not setting them up for scams or burglary.
