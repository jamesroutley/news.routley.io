3: Jacob Trefethen is looking to hire grantmakers for the OpenAI Foundation, and says the roles “may be a good fit for Substackers or Substack lurkers”. He writes:

Here are a few of the program roles:

  • Head of Econ Lab, to lead a team figuring out the economics of transformative AI

  • Program Director, Formal Methods, to support verifiable defense systems as cybersecurity dynamics change

  • Program Officer/Director on High-Burden Diseases, helping channel R&D to fight the diseases most plaguing humanity

  • Program Officer/Director on Catalytic Deployment, figuring out how best to apply AI in the social sector

…and more than a dozen others. Please share with friends who you think may be interested! […] Salary ranges are posted on the job descriptions.

I think these getting the best people in these positions would be good even if you otherwise oppose OpenAI or AI in general; if you disagree with me about this, send me an email arguing why and maybe I’ll avoid boosting the next one.
