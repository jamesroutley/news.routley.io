Sabre Wulf Remastered

Commodore 64 · Genesis Project

Ultimate's 1984 jungle maze, redrawn and reprogrammed for the Commodore 64.

  • C64 PAL
  • 1 player
  • Joystick in port 2
  • D64 disk image

The game

Lost in the jungle

  • 256 screens of jungle. One way out.
  • Find the four pieces of the amulet and show them to the guardian.
  • Stab the small stuff. Run from the big stuff.
  • Flowers give strange powers for a while. Some good, some not.
  • Linger on one screen and a bushfire comes for you.
  • The Sabre Wulf hunts the south. Your sabre won't touch it.
