How to play

  • Every day, four games of seven questions, the same for everyone. 30 seconds per question, 40 in the Abyss.
  • Sky: always give the most common answer. The more popular it is, the higher you climb toward space.
  • Abyss: always give a rare answer. The rarer it is, the deeper you sink toward the trench.
  • Each answer earns up to 100 points: your score is the total, out of 700.
  • Past and Future: give the oldest, or the most recent, answer. It's compared with the other answers to the same question, and each good answer sends you centuries through time.
