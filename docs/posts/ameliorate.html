Using your diagram

Once you've got your diagram, there are a few different ways you can use it.

Grasp your own thoughts

Lay out your ideas with all of their nuance to better think through a problem and make better decisions for yourself.

Solicit feedback

Send out your diagram for others to leave their feedback on - their questions, suggestions, concerns, and opinions.

Guide discussion

Bring your diagram to a meeting to make it easy to keep the discussion focused, knowing precisely where disagreements or uncertainties lie.

Bring others up-to-speed

Present your diagram or let others click around at their own pace to efficiently catch up about the current state of a problem or solution.

Re-visit reasoning

When new information is discovered, update an old diagram and reconsider it anew.

Create decision transparency

Leave behind a scored diagram after a decision is made, as an explanation of why the decision was the way it was.
