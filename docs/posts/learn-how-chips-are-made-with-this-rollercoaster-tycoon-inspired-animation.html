What you are watching

The yellow cart carries one wafer. Its cargo changes at every stop: sand, then rough silicon, then a silver crystal, then a mirror-flat disc, and finally a tray of chips. The middle of the park is a ring, because printing a chip means driving the same six buildings about sixty times, once per layer.
