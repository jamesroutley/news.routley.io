Big Changes

So, we’ve moved back.

Back to the UK.

I had planned on writing this before we moved. In fact, I started the draft in July... but alas. Here we are, two months into living in the UK.

Since I last posted, Mel has gotten into Cambridge University for her PhD. She has finished up her work for the New York City Food Policy Center and is beginning her PhD in Criminology continuing her research on food in prisons this fall. As her program will take a few years, and I did not handle doing long distance for a year well: I have moved with her to Cambridge!

For those who are long time followers of this blog or my life, you know that my first time living in the UK in 2014 & 2015 did not go great. Worked too much, had trouble getting comfortable and meeting people. It was dark times.

But this time will be better! I'll stay on my meds, I won't be alone, and Cambridge is smaller than London. Or at least that's what I'm telling myself.

This is... UK 2: The Revenge Tour.

Our first month was a bit of a shit show. I had problems with my visa, we had problems selling our house and car. But! After five weeks in a hotel, we moved into a nice terrace house in the castle hill area of Cambridge. Mel and I both have small offices, we have a guest room (which you, friend of Nat, are more than welcome to), and a tiny little garden.

I had forgotten how much cheaper telecommunications are in the UK: 2.5Gb symmetrical fiber is £20 a month. Unlimited cell phone data and calls is £25.

I've found some good coffee shops. Slowly figuring out how to get around. Mel has a bike. Mine is in a shipping container coming from the US sometime this century. I'm still working for Laurel, just at much different hours.

Cambridge is a beautiful place. I'm nervous but excited to be here.

So send your love and best wishes on this adventure! Come visit us.

Lots of love from the British Isles,

/Nat
