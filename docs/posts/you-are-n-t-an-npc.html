you are(n't) an npc

24 Nov, 2026

last updated 12 hours, 5 minutes ago

someone replied to my last post with a guestbook entry:

"Being mid is settling for less like being an NPC" - guin

i thought i was pretty clear the first time, but just in case: we are all NPCs, and we all player characters. we are all main character, every day, all day, of our own lives. AND we are all bit character, background extra, super fun/bookish/outdoorsy/ best friend/next door/coworker/love interest/etc side character to someone else!

being mid isn't settling for anything! many of the people you know — all of them, in fact — are mid at most of the things they do! most! i can imagine this makes a certain kind of person cringe, and another type roll their eyes and mutter something about spiky profiles. sure, some people are way better at average at some things, and i'm pretty sure in their quest to be good at this one thing they've learnt they have hopefully accepted aren't destined to be any better than average at almost everything else. hell, i'd say most spiky profile people are probably doing badly in many other areas to maintain their expertise.

to turn the car around pretty forcefully also, NPCs haven't settled for anything, and you, me and everyone we know are not NPCs in the main way that it matters. no one has specifically programmed you/me/anyone you or i know to have certain constraints and whims, and to barely meaningfully exist outside of the purview of the main characters of a video or roleplaying game? free yourself from the idea of "being an NPC" being a thing a human can meaningfully be. there's like eight billion of us rattling around on this planet, i doubt there's been a main character in the heads of every human capable of understanding such a concept since the dawn of humanity, or since the beginning of conscious thought.

being mid — why do i feel like i'm winding up for a manifesto lol — is accepting your human limitations, your thrownness, your friends and family and hopes and dreams, all that squishy stuff, and not letting the bastards get you down about what you aren't or won't be. it's not letting artificial dictates of what you could be, what you might be able to achieve or reach or earn or build, obscure the reality of what is and will be without strain and struggle.

being mid is deciding that being human and normal and average is cool actually, even if you attempt to battle the escape velocity of the limitations of what that means.

being mid is accepting that on your best day you have not "done your best" on every axis a human could possibly work on, because what would that even mean? "doing your best" is a comforting balm and a homing signal all at once. it's your best, only you could do it. it's your best, and that is perfect and precious no matter how amazing or awful it is in the eyes of others.

being mid is wondering at all this and not saying that you are better than or cooler than everyone else, that you are in fact just like the other girls and you want them all to prosper too.

mid is, despite all i just said about best and better and good, about deciding that sometimes good enough is good enough, and using your one wild, precious, fleshy life to worry about the things you do, actually, sincerely care about. or to work out what those things might be!

what's being mid to you? do you feel like an NPC? do you need me to gently shake you through the screen over your answer to the last question? let me know!
