Requests for Hardwares

AI has driven our tolerances for shit software, ridiculous SDKware and bombastic “proprietary” postures to zero.

I’ve never enjoyed being told that I couldn’t do something/enable some feature/use a chip because I wasn’t vetted, or big/funded enough. Nobody has, ever, but now, we fight back, we point Fable at the thing, we tell it “REA1 this basura, give me MCP”.

I am requesting a few hardwares to fulfill my dreams, to bootstart a “"”revolution””” of stuff-that-does-not-get-in-our-doggone-ways.

We need this now because our agents only care if the device is reachable and somewhat documented and not crazy.

Give us Ethernet, accept PoE, stop pretending to be smart. We always just wanted the data, and nobody installed any apps (for their AC, etc.) because they wanted to.

My ‘filter’ is: “a hobbyist would use it for something weird; a startup would need it for something boring.”

  1. A PoE camera, an industrial/robotic/reliable one with focus, zoom, cheap, network controllable, boring/transparent firmware. Let me sense pixels, get out of the way.

    Bonus points: long thin wires. Either the magical single-pair automotive Ethernet that carries power too and is clearly better, thinner, and very-hard-to-find, or some version of GMSL that’s open and cheap. And never tell me “usb” if you don’t want to fight.

  2. A PoE endoscope, a microscopic camera head on a long thin cable that isn’t usb. Yes, it’s the same wish, but make it extremely tiny.

  3. A PDU - a fancy powerbar that lets you (gasp) turn outlets on/off over the Ethernet, that (gasp) lets you measure wattage, and (gasp, gasp) understands WireGuard or Tailscale and is connectable and secure-able. Bonus: not costing $1200 would be the cherry on top.

  4. Audio over Ethernet, cheap. Ask an audio engineer about “Dante”, then understand we need the open, $4 chip version of that. My friend Jessie is working on that, but/and there needs to be infinitely more. Yes, keeping time in sync is tough! Staying alive is also tough - yet you manage. Make it happen.

  5. An extremely small dongle, maybe a single chip, a bead, an RP2350 but smaller, that WireGuards/Tailscales any network device. Not a GL.iNet travel router (they’re fun), an ASIC that every mature hardware manufacturer “just” includes.

Sensors should be DUMB, RELIABLE, and NETWORK-NATIVE, and get out of the way. The brain is now elsewhere (in the weights). Saleae understands this (scroll down to “Logic 2 now speaks MCP”) because they are actually great.
