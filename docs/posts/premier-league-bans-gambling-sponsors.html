Premier league bans gambling sponsors

  • Gambling Sponsorship Ban Implemented: The front-of-shirt gambling sponsorship ban officially took effect for the 2026-27 Premier League season, completely eliminating betting companies from the front of all 20 club jerseys for the first time in many seasons.
  • Shift in Commercial Landscape: The ban has dramatically changed the commercial sponsorship landscape, with finance becoming the new dominant sector, and three clubs (Sunderland, Nottingham Forest, and Chelsea) currently without a front-of-shirt sponsor as a direct or indirect consequence.
  • Alternative Sponsorship Avenues Remain: Despite the front-of-shirt prohibition, gambling brands are still permitted to maintain a visual presence in the Premier League through shirt sleeve sponsorships or training kit branding, as demonstrated by Betano's new deals.

The long-anticipated front-of-shirt gambling sponsorship ban has officially taken effect for the 2026-27 season. For the first time in many consecutive seasons, betting companies are completely absent from the front of match jerseys across all 20 Premier League clubs.

The enforcement of the ban represents a dramatic shift in English football's commercial landscape. Looking back at the 2025-26 campaign, gambling companies held absolute dominance over the league, featuring as the primary sponsor for 11 out of the 20 teams.

From Dominance to Complete Elimination

For 2026-27, every single one of those front-of-shirt betting logos has completely evaporated as the official prohibition comes into full force.

With betting operators pushed off the front of shirts, the commercial breakdown across the Premier League now shows a much more balanced distribution across a variety of global industries:

  • Finance (5 Teams): Takes over as the dominant sector in the league.
  • Airline (3 Teams): Holds the second-largest presence on Premier League chests.
  • Other Sectors: A wide mix of industries have filled the remaining slots, including Technology, Food & Beverage, Human Resources, Software, Insurance, Telecommunications, and Tourism.

Three Clubs Left Without Sponsors

Despite the market opening up, three Premier League teams currently find themselves without a front-of-shirt sponsor as the season approaches: Sunderland, Nottingham Forest, and Chelsea.

  • Sunderland & Nottingham Forest: Both clubs are suffering the direct aftermath of the new regulation, as they have struggled to finalize new commercial deals after being forced to cut ties with their previous gambling sponsors.
  • Chelsea: The London club continues its peculiar ongoing sponsorship saga, entering a fourth consecutive season struggling to secure a stable, long-term front-of-shirt partner.

Pivoting to Sleeves and Training Wear

While front-of-shirt real estate is now strictly off-limits, gambling brands are not disappearing from the Premier League entirely. The current regulations still allow betting companies to maintain a visual presence through shirt sleeve sponsorships or training kit branding.

Betting operator Betano serves as a prime example of this pivot, adapting to the new rules by taking over as Aston Villa's new sleeve sponsor while simultaneously becoming the official training wear sponsor for Tottenham Hotspur.
