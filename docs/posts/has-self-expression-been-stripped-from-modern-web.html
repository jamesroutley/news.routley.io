has self-expression been stripped from modern web?

19 Nov, 2026

last updated 12 hours, 11 minutes ago

does everything sort of look the same to you online right now?

most users of the web mostly operate in walled gardens where we have limited options for self-expression, aka CSS and JS, for the comfort of developers and the safety of users. i miss the cohost model of small, sandboxed experiences that all coalesced in one big timeline, even if i never really took part in it to its fullest.

the pursuit of accessibility is often blamed for everything being the same shape and the same texture or feel online. people make sites that are gloriously old web feeling, full of pomp and whimsy, and refuse to optimise them for mobile or web accessibility. which is their right of course, but feels sad to me. i do understand not every experience can be for everyone, for time, money, skill, energy or can't be bothered reasons.

the corporatisation of the web, with everything contained in the same slick squovals, is also sad to me, as is the recent explosion of intensely obviously AI-aided graphic design. the latter makes me immediately distrust the content, and assume the content of the page is vibe coded too, which is interesting to me. the owners of whimsical sites with autoplaying music and scroll overflows might similarly be flagging themselves to be wholly humanly made, i guess?

some people will never want to build their own sites and services online. some people will never try and make their websites accessible to mobile users, those on low bandwidth connections, or with disabilities.

i think a lot about the sameness of the web on multiple dimensions; substack, for its multitude of faults, allows writers to include audio versions of their posts which i always thought was cool, though i would love to make an experience with interactive transcripts instead of simply an audio player at the top of the page.

for this post i experimented with spake studio, an audio shortform application from omg.lol. i really wanted it to fit in one clip so we'll see how i did!

do you find the web monotonous in look and feel now? let me know!
