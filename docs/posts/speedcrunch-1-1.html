SpeedCrunch 1.1

A free, open-source high-precision scientific calculator.

Built for STEM professionals, students and teachers.

 Download  User Manual  Donate

Free to download and use on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

  • Up to 50 digits of precision, with complex number support
  • 140+ functions, and a myriad of units and constants
  • User-defined variables, functions, and units
  • Vector and matrix support
  • History rewriting, and per-result settings
  • Keypad presets and a fully customizable layout
  • Multiple active sessions with tabs, panes, or windows
  • More than 40 translations, and over 40 color schemes
