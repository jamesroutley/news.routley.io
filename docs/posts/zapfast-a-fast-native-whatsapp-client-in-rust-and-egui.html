ZapFast: A fast, native WhatsApp client in Rust and egui

WhatsApp, native and fast.

Download · Getting started · User guide · Contribute

ZapFast is a WhatsApp client written in Rust with egui and fastframe, the framework behind a family of native desktop apps. It links to your phone as a companion device through whatsapp-rust and has no browser engine. In our Linux test it opens in under a second and uses about 200 MB of idle RAM, against 1.13 GB for WhatsApp Web. See the measurements.

The interface is available in English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Dutch, French, German, Indonesian, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Spanish, and Turkish.

Features · Install · Link your phone · Help

Your chats, on your desktop

Feature What you can do
Messages Send formatted text, replies, edits, reactions, forwards, pictures, files, stickers, GIFs, polls, and voice messages.
Media in the chat Play voice messages, videos, round video messages, GIFs, and animated stickers in place.
Your history Keep your chats in an encrypted archive on this computer, fetch older messages from your phone as you scroll, and export locally saved chat history as text or a ZIP with downloaded attachments.
Several numbers Link more than one WhatsApp number and switch between them in one window.
Desktop controls Use keyboard shortcuts, select and copy text across messages, and stay linked in the tray with notifications.
Themes Choose light, dark, system appearance, or custom colours. On Omarchy, follow your desktop theme.
Privacy Hide locked chats behind a code, or the whole window behind an app lock. Nothing goes to a server of ours.

From source Follow Getting started for dependencies and commands.
  1. Open ZapFast. It shows a QR code.
  2. On your phone, open WhatsApp, then Settings, Linked devices, Link a device, and scan the code. Or click Link with phone number and enter the eight-character code on your phone.
  3. Your recent chats arrive from the phone. Older messages follow as you scroll up.

The link survives restarts, and your phone does not need to stay online to read what ZapFast has already stored. See Getting started for the full walkthrough and How it links for the details.

Report a bug or propose a feature Read Contributing, then use the issue forms.

Read CONTRIBUTING.md before opening an issue or pull request. To look at the interface without a WhatsApp account, run:

cargo run --features demo -- --demo

AGENTS.md describes the architecture, DEMO.md the demo pages, screenshots, and recorded tours, and PACKAGING.md releases and packages.

Native notifications use Fastframe on Linux, macOS and Windows. ZapFast keeps its account/chat routing, privacy settings, notification limits and sound previews. Clicks open the announced message; reading a chat withdraws its tracked notifications. OS permissions and notification presentation still apply.

Want Spotify just as fast and native? Spotifast is ZapFast's sibling. Both are built on fastframe.

ZapFast uses whatsapp-rust, egui, fastframe, the Inter typeface and Noto Color Emoji (OFL), and Lucide icons (ISC), also used for the chat wallpaper doodles. The notification sounds are Pidgin's, under the GPL-2.0 (see assets/sounds/).

ZapFast is an unofficial client and is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta. Using an unofficial client may be against WhatsApp's terms of service and could get an account suspended. Use it at your own risk. WhatsApp is a trademark of WhatsApp LLC.
