Three kinds of inbox

Pick one based on who you're handing the address to.

01 · One-sender

One sender, one address

The first person or company to write claims the address for good. Everyone else is rejected at the door — so a resold or breached list is worth nothing.

Chase Bank ✓ claimed it

Spamco ✕ bounced

List reseller ✕ bounced

chase@yourname.clova.email

created 24 h ✕ after

3 mails in then bounced · self-wipes

02 · Temporary

Gone when you're done

Set a lifetime — an hour for a download link, a month for a trial. When it's up the inbox turns mail away, and can wipe what it holds.

03 · Prefix

Invent one at the counter

Claim a prefix of five characters or more, then anything you add after it already works — no setup, and no dash needed. Each address still locks to its first sender.

maryann -workreceipts_personal^subscription.vet -work @yourname.clova.email

How the three inbox types work together in one mailbox.

Signing in, and your data

Sign in with a Google account, a passkey, or a username and a password. With Google, Clova.Email receives only your email address, your name and your profile photo: the address is how we know it is you, and the name and photo fill in your profile, where you can change or remove them. We never use any of it for advertising, never sell it and never share it. The whole of it is in our privacy policy.
