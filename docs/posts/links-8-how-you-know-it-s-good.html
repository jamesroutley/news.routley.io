links #8: how you know it's good

It's summer in NYC, which means lines — and line discourse. Earlier this month, the Friday right before the long July 4 weekend, I went for a run and thought I'd grab some pastries from nearby Radio Bakery on the way, guessing that the usual line would be shorter since maybe people were out of town. Instead it was the longest I'd ever seen it. It went down the block around the corner and then around the next corner, a 100 people in line for pastries, truly a sight to behold. nymag calls it "the bread line, in some ways" (paraphrasing). Of course I kept on going, right past. And I came back on a random Tuesday morning a couple of weeks later, when there was no line at all. I got two croissants, a stone fruit one and an heirloom tomato one, which were admittedly delicious, all the more so for the smug taste of not having to wait. But don't mistake me for a line hater: I do enjoy standing around with a friend and catching up while waiting for something good to snack on, recently I've been doing something called dosaturday where we get in line for dosas at the Brooklyn Curry Project. Anyway hope this long line (paragraph) of text was worth it, here is line discourse #1 and line discourse #2 (bloomberg, nymag).
