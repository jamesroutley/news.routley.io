IBM gives H-1B, green card holders 3 weeks to relocate from US or leave company

Synopsis

IBM has informed employees with H-1B visas and green cards about the need to relocate within three weeks or depart. Employees who choose not to relocate will receive three months' severance pay, with December 31, 2026, as their last working day. The company's consulting division will no longer sponsor visas and green cards for most positions. India is likely to be the primary destination for relocation opportunities.

IBM has given some of its US-based employees holding H-1B visas and green cards three weeks to decide whether to relocate to another country or leave the company, as the technology giant ends immigration sponsorship for most positions in its consulting division.
