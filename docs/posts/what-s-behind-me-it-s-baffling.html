What's behind me? It's baffling

A while back, on a video call with my family, my mom complained about the amount of echo coming through from my end. I had recently moved; my new office has a lot of hard surfaces. Mom suggested making some wall-hangings to dampen the echo.

She being who she is–well-known knit and crochet designer, author, and teacher Edie Eckman– she offered to design and make a wall hanging, and give me the materials to finish the pair.

Pattern

We discussed a few options for patterns.

I thought it could be fun to have stripes matching TIA-568 colors, i.e. an Ethernet cable pattern.1 But I didn’t want my video call background to be too brightly-colored.

We also talked about encoding the EICAR test string in some fashion, inspired by yomimoni’s beading. But a binary code felt a little too busy.

We settled on a simple pattern of circles scattered across a striped background (formed by varigated yarn). Of course, the circles aren’t randomly scattered; they approximate the EURion constellation.2

I don’t expect the anti-copying properties of EURion to come into effect here, but I’ll be amused if they do. I thought it appropriate to use an anti-counterfitting tool for a baffle.

The pattern is almost entirely double-crochet stitches. All of the difficulty came in putting them in the right place: each ring embeds in a 3x5 pattern of chain/stitch/chain in the underlying fabric, and the spacing of those is irregular by design. Making the fabric was very much an excercise in counting, re-counting, and frogging when I got the placement wrong. Thanks as always to Edie for clear and accurate directions!

Mounting

This is the project I alluded to in a footnote on this post. While most of the work in this project was crocheting, I also laser-cut a little bit of mounting hardware.

On the back of each hanging, chains of 5 single-crochets zig-zag across the top and bottom edges. When mounted, these attach the hanging to a horizontal dowel, which slides through the mounting brackets. It’s not super sturdy, but the hangings aren’t too heavy; good enough!
