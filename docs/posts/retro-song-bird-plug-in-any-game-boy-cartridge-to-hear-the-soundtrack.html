Retro Song Bird: Plug in any Game Boy cartridge to hear the soundtrack

Plug in any Game Boy® or Game Boy Color® cartridge to hear the soundtrack of your classic games.

Step 1.

That's it. Enjoy.

Music

Supports 1,900+ games. Plug in your favorite and let the soundtrack teleport you back in time.

  • Every track, plus sound effects
  • Controls for next, previous and volume
  • Stereo line out for your hi-fi
  • Accurate, register-level sound emulation
  • Plays homebrew game soundtracks made with GB Studio

Archiving

Because art belongs in a museum, Retro Song Bird helps you preserve the carts that matter to you.

  • Back up and restore save files
  • Back up ROMs
  • Flash writable carts
  • Export any soundtrack as WAV

Chiptunes

Dig through the clearance bin: every cart is a gem when you can remix its original instrument stems.

  • Each of the four sound channels (two square, wave, noise) as its own USB audio stream, ready for your DAW
  • Plug in an LSDj cart to play back your saved songs
  • Sync two Retro Song Birds over MIDI, set the tempo to beat-match, and DJ a set
  • Releasing an album? GBAlbum turns it into a ROM that plays on real hardware and Retro Song Bird

Image

USB Webcam

Plug in a Game Boy Camera® and Retro Song Bird becomes a USB webcam. Join your next meeting in beautiful 0.014 megapixels.

  • 128×112 pixels
  • Four shades, classic dither
  • ~5 frames per second? Look, it's a bonus feature.

...and More

  • Alarm Mode wakes you with any cartridge. Press the button to snooze, or pull the cart to stop
  • Play (and cheat) in your browser, with Game Genie and GameShark codes
  • Build Mixtapes from across your collection and flash them to writable carts to woo your crush, '80s style
  • A history of every cart you've ever plugged in
  • An open API for developers: read, flash and back up carts, grab Game Boy Camera® frames, and more, all via web requests
  • Over-the-air updates add features and supported carts, so it keeps getting better

How it works

  1. 1

    Insert Cart

    Any Game Boy® or Game Boy Color® cartridge goes in the slot on top.

  2. 2

    Read ROM

    Retro Song Bird has a map of 1900+ carts and knows which sections of the ROM to read.

  3. 3

    Synth

    A register-accurate emulation of the Game Boy sound engine is run on the relevant parts of the ROM to produce glorious chiptunes.
