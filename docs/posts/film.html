Fractured Frame (2025)

How To Be Human (2025)

An otherworldly being takes human form and comes to see that humanity is shaped by the uncertain search for truth.

A 2.5-minute experimental film made entirely without a camera or microphones in only 18 hours.

My role: Co-director and editor.

  • 🏆 Winner, The Big Screen Hack: 18-hour AI Filmmaking Hackathon
  • Premiered at RecDek @ the BFI, Stephen Street, London, during the BFI London Film Festival (October 2025)
