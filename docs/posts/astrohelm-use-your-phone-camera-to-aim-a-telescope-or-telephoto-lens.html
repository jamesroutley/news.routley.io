Personalized guide to the night sky

AstroHelm comes with a curated list of great objects to observe and highlights the best ones for your location and time. From bright planets to faint galaxies and nebulae, AstroHelm helps you find what’s worth seeing.

Pick, follow, and discover

Choose an object and AstroHelm shows you which way to move your telescope. Your phone’s camera recognizes the stars overhead and continuously determines where your telescope is pointing with high precision, ensuring the object lands right in your eyepiece.

No astronomy knowledge required

No need to learn star hopping or memorize constellations. And unlike a GoTo mount, there’s no complicated multi-star alignment. Better yet, AstroHelm can find its way even under heavily light-polluted skies.

Observatory-Grade Precision in Your Pocket

Traditional astronomy apps rely on your phone's compass and gyroscope, which drifts and lags. AstroHelm takes a picture of the night sky and matches it against a massive database of stars, so it always knows where you are pointing.
