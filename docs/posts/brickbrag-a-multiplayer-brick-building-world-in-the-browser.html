Show HN: Brickbrag – A multiplayer brick-building world in the browser

NEWUpdated Oct 13 · 7 new things →

A whole town made of bricks – with a race track, an airfield, buses out to the volcano and room for a house of your own. Shared live with everyone online.

  • Phone & computer
  • Nothing to download
  • No sign-up

Meet me at the rainbow B.

The middle of town turns around a giant rainbow letter. Signposts point to every corner of the world, the lookout tower catches the last sun, and the café always has a free table.

Go shopping. Go fast.

Two stops right off the ring road: one for bricks, one for wheels.

The Brick Store

Fill your pockets. Bricks in every color – and every part the town itself is built from.

The car dealer

Pick a car, drive it off the lot and take your friend for a spin.

One more lap. Then one more.

The race track starts at a gantry with a live board: today’s fastest laps, everyone’s name on it. Beat the time, take the top spot – until someone beats you.

Cleared for takeoff.

The tower blinks, the hangars are full of trikes – small flying machines anyone can steer. Roll down the runway, pull up, and see the whole world from above.

Your bus driver is a robot. He’s never late.

Four bus lines leave the city for the wild. Hop on, sit back and watch the world roll by – and at the last stop, walk on into the flower fields.

Out there, it gets wild.

Past the edge of town the world keeps going: lava, snow, sand and blossoms. Walk, drive, fly or take the bus.

    Color as far as you can see

Build it brick by brick. Keep it.

Grab the bricks and start anywhere. Build together in public, or buy a lot of your own – only you build there, and it stays yours.

Stud by stud

Hundreds of bricks in every color. Snap them together, turn them, take them back – a house, a tower, whatever you like.

Land of your own

The Land Office sells lots. Put your name on one and build what you want – nobody can knock it down.

Everyone you meet is real.

Well – except the bus driver. Say hi, chat, send a gift of coins and bricks, race your friends, or just hang out by the B and watch the town go by.

Straight from the game.

Every picture here is the game as you play it, in your browser. Tap one to look closer.

How to play

Every button is on screen when you need it. This is the short version.

W A S D
Walk, mouse to look

Space
Jump – twice for a double jump

Click
Place a brick, right-click takes it back

R C
Turn the brick, pick a color

V
Change the view

E
Get into a buggy or plane

L
Your land

T
Chat

Tab
Who is online, send gifts

Esc
Menu and all controls
