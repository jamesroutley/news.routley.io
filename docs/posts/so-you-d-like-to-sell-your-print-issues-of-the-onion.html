How To Identify Your Issues.

To check whether you have the issues we’re seeking, look at the Volume and Issue Numbers. Every issue of The Onion has a volume number, an issue number, and a date. This information is all listed on the front page, but in different spots depending on the issue itself. Special issues may list the volume & issue numbers as “22-01” instead of “Volume 22 Issue 1”. Have Some? Tell Us.

Let us know if you have any of the requested issues by completing the Google Form and providing as much information as you can. Our archivist will reach out to you if your collection is of interest to us. The more of our gaps you can fill, the more interested we will be.
