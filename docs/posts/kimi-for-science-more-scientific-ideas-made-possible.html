Kimi for Science: More scientific ideas, made possible

Science has always been driven by human curiosity, rigorous inquiry, and a passion for the unknown

A valuable hypothesis can require extensive literature review, data analysis, and repeated validation. An unexpected result can also spark the next breakthrough. Yet limited time, compute, and people mean many ideas worth exploring never receive the attention they deserve. We believe AI can accelerate research and expand the boundaries of discovery, helping researchers move beyond constraints of time, resources, and methodology and bringing more ambitious ideas within reach

From life sciences and health to energy and materials, and from fundamental science to the unknown, countless important questions remain unanswered. We hope Kimi can be a trusted partner for scientists, supporting researchers as they take on harder, more original problems

Give every bold idea the chance to be tested, and turn more discoveries of lasting value into reality

Who we’re looking for

Kimi for Science welcomes researchers across the natural sciences, engineering, and related interdisciplinary fields to apply

We welcome research at every stage, from improving existing workflows with Kimi to exploring new methodologies and tackling more challenging scientific problems

What we offer

More resources for your research

Selected applicants will receive up to $20,000 in Kimi API credits (approx. 7B tokens), with the exact amount determined by research needs and review outcomes.

Dedicated technical and research resources

Technical support from the Kimi team, plus early access to new models and curated research and community resources

Long-term partnership opportunities

Outstanding researchers and teams can access longer-term, more in-depth technical and resource support
