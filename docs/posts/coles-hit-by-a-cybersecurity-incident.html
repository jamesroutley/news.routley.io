Coles hit by a cybersecurity incident

Statement

Current as at 16 November 2026, 8:30am

Coles Group Limited is currently investigating a cyber security incident.

Based on the findings of our investigation to date, we have not identified any evidence that customer, team or supplier data has been compromised or removed from Coles’ systems.

Coles is working with the Australian Cyber Security Centre and will continue to engage with appropriate agencies and regulators as investigations continue.

Coles is also working with global cyber security experts to contain and secure the Coles IT environment.

Operations continue and customers are able to shop as normal with Coles in stores and online.

We apologise for any concern this may cause our customers and key stakeholders at a time of increasing cyber incidents in Australia and around the world. We will provide further updates as appropriate on this page.
