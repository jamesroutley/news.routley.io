Freshening up my website

Published on Sat Oct 17, 2026

Taken in Kyoto, Japan. September 2026.

I’ve had a bit of free time lately which I’ve dedicated to a long-overdue rewrite of my website! Most things should look pretty much the same as they did before. The most notable user-facing changes are a new photos page, a hierarchical navbar, a zillion tiny pixel and color pushes, and, assuming I didn’t miss something, every page now starts with a photo I’ve taken.

The bulk of the changes were behind the scenes. The guts of my previous website were getting pretty rusty, with a lot of half-assed abandoned experiments sitting around making it difficult to focus on what I really wanted to use this space for: sharing my ideas, knowledge, and photos. I’m still rocking jekyll to render everything, but this time I’ve replaced HTML/Liquid templates with slim. For whatever reason I’ve chosen to die on the hill of continuing to write and maintain my own HTML and CSS in the era of clankers, but I at least made it easier on myself by clearing out all the < and >s. It’s such an improvement over constantly fumbling with logic in Liquid templates1. Trying to bolt a stripped-down programming language into HTML always felt dirty to me. I’m glad Slim just gives me Ruby and lets me fling filth of my own making around my layouts.

Anyways, today was a milestone for me so I figured I’d cap off the work with a post to celebrate. My hope is that this new site ends up unclogging a bunch of things I’ve been meaning to publish but have consistently put off due to how much of a hassle it was to build and deploy my last site.

Long live the small web!
