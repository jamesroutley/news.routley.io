MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display and Touch

Design

Everything to see here.

Introducing MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display. Featuring a groundbreaking tandem OLED display with Touch, Dynamic Island, a redesigned thermal system, an enhanced audio system, and slimmer display borders — all in a lighter design. It’s the pinnacle of pro.

  • Sizes and weights. The 14-inch model weighs just 3.0 pounds and the 16-inch model weighs just 4.0 pounds.

  • Colors. Available in two stunning finishes. Shown in Space Black.

  • Display. The spectacular PROLED XDR display features an industry-leading tandem OLED panel that provides up to 2000 nits peak HDR and SDR brightness, a 10,000,000:1 contrast ratio, and a wider color gamut covering both P3 and Adobe RGB.

  • Touch. Get hands-on with Multi-Touch gestures. Scroll, pinch, rotate, draw, and more — right onscreen.

  • Speakers and mics. The best audio system in a laptop. A redesigned six-speaker sound system delivers improved clarity and 50 percent deeper bass. And a studio-quality three-mic array makes sure you come through loud and clear.

  • Camera. The 12MP Center Stage camera keeps you in frame as you move around. Desk View lets you share your workspace. And advanced video stabilization lets you touch the screen without causing your camera feed to shake.

  • Connectivity. Three Thunderbolt 5 ports, an HDMI port, a MagSafe 3 port, an SDXC card slot, a headphone jack, Wi‑Fi 7, and Bluetooth 6. Connect high-speed peripherals, transfer photos, work with multiple external displays, and power up instantly.

PROLED XDR Display

Brilliance

PROLED XDR combines breakthrough tandem OLED technology with advanced algorithms in the display engine, delivering Extreme Dynamic Range and stunning visual fidelity for the ultimate front-of-screen performance. As a reference-caliber display, it delivers outstanding precision for filmmaking, design, and even medical imaging. With unparalleled brightness and contrast, deeper blacks, and an exceptionally wide color gamut, you’ll immediately see why it’s the world’s best laptop display.

Security

No compromises.

Security starts with Apple silicon and extends to the macOS architecture. This deep integration of hardware and software, along with automatic updates and built-in antivirus protection, helps keep MacBook Pro stable and secure for the long term. At the heart of MacBook Pro is the Secure Enclave, a dedicated security subsystem that’s separate from the main processor. It keeps your most sensitive data, including encryption keys and biometric information, fully isolated from the rest of the system — the same protection that powers Touch ID and Find My.
