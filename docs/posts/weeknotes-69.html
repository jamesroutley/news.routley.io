Less computer is about less computer of all types. It is an intentional effort to reduce dependence on computational thinking and infrastructure. To be an informed participant in conversations about computers is to persistently challenge the use of any kind of computers, which leads to the best uses where they fit, and a reduction in all kinds of headaches and time commitments and power imbalances when they don't. - less computer, know the limits of technical fixes
