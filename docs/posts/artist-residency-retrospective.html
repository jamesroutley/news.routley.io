Artist Residency: Retrospective

Follow-up to my last post, Artist Residency: Day 1

Ok. Okayyyyy. I didn't blog at all during it1Or for weeks afterwards., but I had a great time on my residency! It was healing to get away from things and, as they say, touch grass. Did you know that it feels good to go walk in the woods? Wild.

Anyway, it was a beautiful time, and I highly recommend anyone who has a chance to do something similar to take it. In the time since I've fallen back into some of my same old bad habits, but such is life. I'll leave you with the main thing I worked on during my time there, a painting of the rock garden outside my studio space.
