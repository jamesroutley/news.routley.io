What’s new in Glyphs 4?

Pen points

Flex your designs with variable strokes.

Turn any path into a living shape. Drop pen points on a segment to command width, height, and angle—all adapting perfectly to the path’s direction emitting smooth transitions to the next node.

Pointed-nib design by Giuseppe Salerno

COLRv1 support

Colors, blends, gradients. You got it.

A piece of colored cake: combine colored shapes with blending modes and opacities and achieve smoother gradients. Customize everything, see it live. Export as images—or COLRv1 fonts.

Visual kerning groups

Stop thinking. Build visually.

Kerning has just become fun. Drag and drop glyphs straight onto the shelf to build your kerning groups. Instantly organize your sets and keep your font’s kerning structured perfectly.

New sidebar

Say goodbye to changing tabs.

Edit view now features a powerful left sidebar that puts your most critical tools in immediate reach. Filter your glyph set, walk through your shapes, inspect your interpolations right away.

Stroke caps

Control stroke miters and endings.

Pick caps and corners for your monoline strokes, batch-edit with ease. Enjoy the many more options at your fingertips.

New Slanter filter

Make it oblique, now without the hassle.

Set slant, adjust contrast, shape and weight corrections, and boom, there is your oblique. Try it in a glyph or two, go on to apply your tweaks on the whole font, done.

New component type

Build better icons with head components.

Want to do more than the default round and cut-off stroke butts? Create a _head glyph, add the anchors (⌘U), and then add the head component to any open stroke ending with the context menu. This way, you can efficiently reuse shapes and build consistent and ultra-editable icon systems.

All-new scripting window

Write code with a little help from your friends.

Navigate and edit all installed Python scripts and plug-ins, launch dedicated debug sessions, complete with breakpoints to pause your code where it cracks. And make Glyphs help you stay style-consistent with built-in helpers.

Browse your font masters

Massive families, zero friction.

Navigating complex font families should not slow you down. Use the new master selector for grouping, filtering and browsing your masters in one place. Instantly find the exact properties you are looking for, switch masters on the fly and take control of your project without losing your focus.

Ordina by Fadhl Haqq

Live text preview

See your fonts in action, no detours.

Put your designs to the test in the revamped text preview window, without leaving the app. Try different alignments, trackings and leadings, flip through your instances, change colors, even test your OpenType features right here.

Star nodes

Take your curves from smooth to perfect.

Shift-click a node to turn it into a star node—and get a flawless G2 transition from curve to curve. Making icons? Use the star node on the transitions from curve to line, and you can achieve the squircle shape for your app symbol.

Discover your tribe

Integrate hundreds of plug-ins built for, and by, the Glyphs community

Kern On

Tim Ahrens

Kern on is a revolutionary kerning assistant that helps you cut down the time you spend kerning to just a few minutes. It takes care of kern groups and your kerning. All it needs is a few kern pairs from you, then the plug-on ‘kerns on for you’.

Speed Punk

Yanone

Speed Punk is a learning tool. It teaches you to better understand the nature of Bézier curves and their curvature, the technical basis of digital type design.

Font Remix Tools

Just Another Foundry

The Font Remix Tools are a set of plug-ins Glyphs. They allow to harmonize glyph shapes, tune the width, automatically generate small caps, generate superior figures and many more.

Lttr/Ink

Martin Cetkovský and Filip Zajac

Use stroke skeletons to speed up your work. LTTR/INK’s mighty nib force lets you draw with skeletons and control the stroke like never before. Forget outline editing, LTTR/INK generates perfectly plotted outlines, even directly to the exported font file.
