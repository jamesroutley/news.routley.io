it is always the time of monsters

i'll stop bastardising this misattributed - and maybe mangled? - quote at some point, but not today. this post is kinda a reply to myself. i enjoyed this post about how the internet is alive due to all the egoists posting through it and i enjoyed a different post on blogging and how one stays committed to it despite the horrors.

i think it's true that everything is dying all the time, here are some current highlights:

  • libraries!
  • literacy in general!
  • democracy!
  • reading as a hobby!
  • the internet!
  • blogging!
  • social media depending on who you ask!
  • popular internet lexicography/useful spellcheck!

i think some of the things on this list are things are being murdered, honestly. they are not natural, lack of interest based deaths. but you can't rely on anything being here forever, because nothing will be. crunch or heat death, the end of everything is coming. sure, it's more on the billions of years scale of things, but it barrels towards us nonetheless.

this used to terrify me to no end and i'm not exactly sure what changed. i think knowing that so long as the sun shines and life finds a way there will always be new vistas of exploration, even if i don't see more than a sliver of them with my own eyes, is helpful. in terms of the internet, knowing there are other egoists, hobbyists, nerds, and weirdos who will wake up on a clear and sunny day with no particular plans, grab a glass of something seasonal and decide to write/code/create/go ONLINE, i am less afraid of everything being lost here than i've ever been.

people are raging against the dying of the light on every corner! the darkness may be inevitable, but we will go down fighting every time.

this is my sixteenth post in my writing month challenge, which i decided is "one post for each day of november".
