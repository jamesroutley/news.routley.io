    Tri Cities Airport Basement

    Blountville, TN

    This restroom, or what's left of it, is located on the basement level of the Tri Cities Regional Airport. Despite its appearance, the restroom is unlocked and accessible to the public. This was originally a secondary restroom located near a storage space, likely only for workers when it was functional. The toilets and urinals have since been stripped away, and what remains are old pipes and a crusty sink. The windows lead to shafts that open up to the street level while a lone hole under the sink leads down a dark and empty crawl space, corroded by time
