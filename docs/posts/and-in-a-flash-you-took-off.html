and in a flash, you took off

HomeBlog

09 Aug, 2026

you come for water on these hot days.

in the mornings, you've let me know your thoughts

as I fill your cup.

but

on this day,

as I sit in my loop,

something happened.

I don't know what scared you,

but you hit,

and you fell.

I ran out and saw you

and I held you

and I sobbed.

your little short feathers - precious.

your reds and yellows and browns - brilliant.

your little crossed bill - beautiful – but hurt.

your little dense body filled my hands –

with heat

and softness

and after some time

and

many

tears

– all at once –

as if you came from a deep slumber

you awoke

you moved your head and

our

eyes

met

and in a flash, you took off.

my heart soared.

I dreamed of you and worried for your little head.

but in the morn, you sat upon the wire.

you called in your flights between the fir and the dogwood.

and then as I gardened, you came where I filled your cup.

With a mark upon your beak, I know you. I held you.

You looked at me today, and my heart was full.
