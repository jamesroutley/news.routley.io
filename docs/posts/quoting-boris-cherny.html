there are these days that the ocean calls you to her vastness and depths.

and so, you find yourself moved to journey to her edge.

starting with a stream, you make your way along the estuary.

first, you encounter the sound of splashing and feathers

and witness the young and old coming together

in a joyful

celebration

of water,

and

warmth.

as you pass,

the water becomes still.

you reflect

and are

reflected.

as you walk,

you wander,

you take in all

the

little

grains

of sand,

and you find that

you

are also taken in.

you find texture

everywhere.

you dip your toes

into

the

salty

edge –

where two worlds meet.

and then, make your way back

along the stream – occasionally looking back.

Once you come back to the forest,

you start again,

but above the vastness,

that comes to the shores,

lined with light.

you find that beneath the surface

there

is

beauty

and the

child

of

beauty.

You make

your

way

down

to the cobbles.

And find radiance.

and the child

of

radiance.

When it is time you touch the waters,

and

move back

up.

you are overwhelmed with gratitude

for a sea of beaks that sits at the top –

for this year they

are more present

in this changing world.

you relax into the cliffs at

this

edge

of the world

and witness.

your heart

fills.

and then you breathe in.

you look out.

and you return from this edge of the earth.
