Show HN: Olumino, a Markdown notes and spaced repetition app

Write notes, turn them into flashcards, and review each card on a schedule based on how well you know it.

The research behind it

In 1885, Hermann Ebbinghaus showed that we forget most of what we learn within days, and that each review makes the memory last longer.1 Reviewing at growing gaps, called spaced repetition, is one of the most reliable ways to remember what you study.

Olumino schedules every card with FSRS-7, an open method trained on millions of real reviews.2 It estimates how well you know each card and brings it back right as you start to forget.

  1. 1. Ebbinghaus, H. (1885). Über das Gedächtnis. Leipzig: Duncker & Humblot.
  2. 2. Ye, J., Su, J. and Cao, Y. (2022). A Stochastic Shortest Path Algorithm for Optimizing Spaced Repetition Scheduling. KDD '22. Open Spaced Repetition

How it shows what you know

New

A card you haven't studied yet starts as a new moon.

Learning

Memories fade without review. A card you just learned comes back within a day or two.

Getting stronger

Each time you remember a card, the gap before its next review grows, from days to weeks to months.

Full

A full moon means you remembered the card after three weeks away and it's likely to last a month or more. It still comes back for review now and then.

Studying

A few minutes a day. Swipe each card, and Olumino decides when you'll see it again.

For teachers

Make a class and share the link. Your students get your decks, and you can see who studied this week and which cards the class keeps forgetting. It's free for teachers and students.

Questions

Is Olumino free?

Yes. Decks, cards, notes and studying are free. Syncing between devices is part of Olumino Plus, $8 a month.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Olumino runs in your browser on a computer, tablet or phone. On a phone you can add it to your home screen, and it opens like an app.

Where are my cards and notes kept?

On your device first. If you sign in, a copy is kept in your account so your other devices get it, and only you can see it. When card suggestions are on, the paragraph you're writing is sent so suggestions can be made. You can turn them off in Settings.

Do I have to use the suggested cards?

No. You can write every card yourself, and nothing is added unless you add it.

How does it decide when to show a card?

It uses FSRS-7, the newest version of an open spaced repetition method trained on millions of real reviews. Each answer you give (Forgot, Recalled or Instant) changes when the card comes back. Cards you know well come back less often, and cards you miss come back sooner.

What do the moons mean?

Each card has a moon that fills as you learn it, from a new moon (never studied) to a full moon (likely to last a month or more). A moon only grows after you remember the card following time away.

What happens to cards I keep forgetting?

A card you forget three times in one session goes to Revisit, where you can practice it on its own. Get it right twice in a row and it goes back to normal.

Can I bring my Anki decks, or take my cards somewhere else?

Yes. You can import Anki decks with their review history. You can also export any deck to Anki, a spreadsheet, Quizlet, Markdown or a printable sheet.

Can I keep a copy of everything?

Yes. Settings has a button that saves all your decks, cards, notes and pictures to one file, and you can open it again on any device.

Can I use Olumino to learn new languages?

Yes. Cards can be read aloud in 16 languages and accents, and Japanese has extra tools: readings over kanji, a dictionary and writing practice.
