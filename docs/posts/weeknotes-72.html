  • hey, hello, hi! it's weeknotes time!
  • i'm wiped for no good reason and feeling a little ill so we'll see how this goes! i think i'll probably feel better tomorrow, v mild, but still.
  • trying to think of ways to prompt myself to write down every thought and noticing and recollection and not assume i'll do it later/forget that is even an option. used to be more of a note pack rat, and obviously i do still take a fair amount of planner style notes, but original thought notes are lacking in my opinion. trying to expand and add a bit every day but so far telling myself to do that isn't working, so any PKM or commonplacing nerds out there, please let me know what you do!
  • through a planning system i mentioned briefly in passing in a post this week, i realised that even if i don't have (m)any "traditional" habits (done at the same time every day with a clear trigger, motivation and outcome, james clear atomic habits style), i have a lot of daily and longer time period rhythms, and really suck at incorporating more one time actions and "one time actions that all conglomerate to make a unified end product" style endeavours. it's an interesting realisation, i always thought i despised all habits! it also reveals why so many of my days are feeling vaguely humdrum at the moment, and why i always want to make everything (cleaning, exercise, video making, blogging as you can see, note-making, reading) into a daily portable routine!
  • continuing with writing month! i am shocked which posts do "well" and which do not! still mostly enjoying myself but have to remember that if i want to write an ambitious post i actually have to start the day before or early in the day, research takes time, who knew?
  • mind slightly blown by the idea that trying harder is a thing you can learn to do (pardon the substack)? not everyone should or wants to try harder and i think that is their birthright, but if you want to freely i latently thought that the amount of effort one can apply was ordained by personality and fixed lol? a revelation.
  • i think i probably will have to go for CS primer in the end despite it being eye wateringly expensive for a subscription. i think i'll trial for a month and if i don't end up doing any of the exercises or opening my computer science quaternary eye a little i'll just cancel and find something else.
  • really enjoyed all the replies to my post "what's the point", james' in particular gets to the heart of what most of the posts say, because it's fun being online!
  • having lots of "damn, this year has passed in the blink of an eye!" thoughts but also i have done a lot of the stuff i was trying to do this year! always a lil grief for that which is left undone but trying not to let it overshadow the hectic gainz!
  • i've been eating shawarma wraps, burgers, pasta with bolognese and ham sandwiches. i've been drinking london fog, crispy cold vimto squash and chai lattes.
  • i hope you've had a fantastic week and next week treats you so well. 💟
  • see you next time?
