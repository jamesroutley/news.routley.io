CrossPoint Reader

Your books, on a reader you can make your own. CrossPoint is free software for compact e-readers, built by the people who read on them.

Join the thousands of readers who love CrossPoint

More control over how you read.

CrossPoint is the software behind the screen. Free to install, with new features built by people who use it every day.

EPUB formatting and typography

Read EPUBs with justified text, language-aware hyphenation, and working footnotes. CrossPoint also renders drop caps, small caps, and illustrations.

Read the reading guide →

Custom fonts and page layout

Choose your typeface, size, margins, and line, word, and letter spacing. Convert a font you own with our font builder, or load TrueType fonts directly on readers with external RAM.

Set up custom fonts →

Library search and sorting

Browse every book on your SD card. Search by title or author, and sort by recent reads, title, or author.

Read the library guide →

Wi-Fi transfers, Calibre, and OPDS

Drop a book into your browser or send it from Calibre. Browse OPDS and OPDS 2.0 catalogs on the reader. Away from home? Load books through its own Wi-Fi hotspot.

Get books onto your reader →

Highlights, bookmarks, and reading sync

Highlight a passage, save a clipping, or name a bookmark. Quick Resume takes you straight back into your book, and KOReader sync keeps your place across devices.

Use highlights and clippings →

Offline dictionaries

Look up a word or a whole phrase without leaving your book. Add a StarDict dictionary and use it offline, wherever you’re reading.

Add a dictionary →

Multilingual and right-to-left text

Community translations, right-to-left text, and hyphenation rules that follow the language of your book. Indic scripts aren’t supported yet.

Explore language support →

Focus Reading and page-turn controls

Try Focus Reading for a guided pace. On supported hardware, turn pages with a tilt or set up your own tap and swipe gestures.

Set up Focus Reading →

Bluetooth page turners

Pair a compatible Bluetooth remote to turn pages without touching your reader. Assign short and long presses to page turns, chapter navigation, the reader menu, or clipping selection.

Pair your remote in Settings → Connectivity → Bluetooth Page Turner, then choose what each button does.
