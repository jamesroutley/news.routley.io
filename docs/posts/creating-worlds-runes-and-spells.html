Creating Worlds: Runes and Spells

When we make worlds, there are maps, star charts, languages, fashion, technology, currencies, governments, peoples, and so much more. This post is about creating a magic system. More posts will follow on world-building.

We're going to make a magic system of runes and spells that can be cast on the runes. This is but one approach of many. I made a prototype toy for this activity if you wish to play. I recommend spell books be made with ink on paper under a full moon.

Runes

A rune is a symbol, a glyph, an object that you give power.

Now that we know how to make runes, write spells, and cast them, let's make some spell books. Let's define a spell book as closed when the spells and runes within can only generate the runes within (no surprise runes).
