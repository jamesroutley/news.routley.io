why i oppose self driving vehicles by laura michet

The simplest example I can think of is "Waymo runs over a child." Usually, when a car strikes a kid, insurance companies and local police handle the incident - often very badly. Imagine, however, that someone with infinite wealth or power runs over a young person. In an incident where a car with far more lawyers and wealth than any insurance company strikes a child, I am sure that the family of that kid would not appreciate the power imbalance. Imagine having to go up against Google or Amazon to get justice for vehicular violence? In a country where killing someone with your car is already basically legal?

owning my streaming with navidrome by will richardson

I won’t rehash the content of the video, but I didn’t feel like I was getting value out of my Spotify subscription, since I don’t spend that much time listening to music. Because of the way the artists’ pay out is calculated, most of my subscription would be going to artists I don’t listen to. My understanding is that all the subscriptions are pooled, Spotify takes their cut, and then the rest is divvied up to artists based on what portion of the overall streaming time was for their music. So if you don’t listen very much, your streaming time won’t move the needle on how much your favourite bands contribute to the total listening time across all of Spotify.

how i cancelled my spotify subscription by v buckenham

I have been on a kick of unbundling all the things I use my phone for onto separate devices. I would like to claim that it's because I'm trying to be mindful, because it means I use social media less, that I am generally a more virtuous person, stepping free of the fuss and bother of modern life. But actually I have to admit, it's because I like a gadget.

how the month in chile went re: POTS and how i write with POTS from the white pube

I guess in this text about how I write with POTS, let me be clear that sometimes there’s no writing at all. I have more good days than bad at the moment, so I’m going to outline what a good day looks like. This is for the beloved people of the salt, because god, a lot of preparation and maintenance goes into keeping that long list of symptoms at bay (or trying to, anyway).

the complex grief of gen AI by Tracey Durnell

While I’ve taken basic steps to block my writing from the Machine, I get generated comments several times a week that were clearly fed my writing. I have refused to allow the knowledge that corporations are thieves and scammers are scum stop me from publishing. There is dignity, I think, in both refusal, as I’ve chosen, and retreat, as this person has chosen — though both are mixed with grief.
