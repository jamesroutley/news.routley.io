Network Automation for the Modern Age

Yesterday I gave a talk at DENOG 18 called “Network Automation for the Modern Age”. Marcus Stögbauer and I gave a primordial version of this talk at this year’s DKNOG, but since then much has happened.

It also is the first official cyberwitchery talk, and the recording is already up on media.ccc.de, with a mirror on YouTube.

Fig. 1: Me, talking about the edges.

It’s about putting your own data model at the center of your network automation, with alembic as the case study and a live demo in the middle.

I hope you like it, I was honestly quite pleased with how it turned out. If any of this sounds relevant to your network, or you’d like me to give a version of the talk somewhere, let us know!
