Ashland Nature Center

At the start of October I picked up a new lens: a Tamron 150-500mm f/5-6.7. I was just in time to visit the Ashland Nature Center for their hawk watch, monitoring migrating birds.

The bird feeders were, as always, a great source of visible birds. I can’t say I got great photos of the hawks; I’m still working on my birds-in-flight technique and getting used to the new lens. The extra zoom and aperture of the Tamron compared to the Nikon 28-400mm are definitely nice, but the extra weight is definitely a workout.

Woodpeckers

Various sorts of woodpeckers were out in force.

Tennessee warbler

There was a large group (four or more) of Tennessee warblers that visited the bird feeders. Many birders complain about warblers, but these were happy to stay in sight for quite a while.

Hawk watch

Each fall, birders and naturalists keep watch for migrating hawks from the top of a hill at Ashland. The location has clear sightlines for quite a ways.
