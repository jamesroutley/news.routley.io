Apple removed my open-source app from EU App Stores after 9 years

Hi everyone!

For those who don't know me, let me introduce myself. I'm the independent developer behind FSNotes, an open-source note-taking app for macOS and iOS.

I've been developing FSNotes for 9 years. And what happened a few weeks ago came as a complete shock.

Apple removed my app from every European App Store. Without a clear explanation or any way to resolve the issue promptly.

It all started when I didn't receive my October sales payout. This had happened before, but I was usually able to resolve it by contacting Apple Support. Not this time.

I opened two support cases:

  • November 5 — Case ID: 23353628
  • November 17 — Case ID: 23573022

Since November 5, I haven't received a single response. Not one.

I've gone through everything I could find in App Store Connect, completed the necessary compliance information under the EU Digital Services Act (DSA), and submitted official documents from Ukraine's government business registry.

I'm a legally registered sole proprietor in Ukraine. I run my business officially and pay my taxes. But apparently, that's not enough. Or perhaps nobody is even reviewing my documents.

And you know what's the most frustrating part?

While Apple remains silent, everything I've spent nine years building is being destroyed.

My app is losing its positions in App Store rankings and search results. The visibility, reputation, and discoverability I've worked so hard to build over the years are disappearing right before my eyes.

Even if Apple restores my app tomorrow, who's going to give me those rankings back? Who will compensate me for the lost time, users, and revenue?

I'm not asking for special treatment. I'm not asking Apple to bend the rules for me. I simply want someone to review my documents, explain the restrictions, and give me a chance to resolve the issue.

It's especially painful to see how difficult it has become for a Ukrainian developer to keep working. Sometimes it feels like Ukraine is the country under sanctions, not Russia.

FSNotes is still available for free on GitHub. I'll continue providing access to the source code and free builds. But the reality is that without revenue, it will be extremely difficult for an independent project like this to survive.

I'm asking you to support this open letter to Apple with a like or a repost. I'm not asking for donations or special privileges. I just want to bring this situation to the attention of the App Store team.

You cannot simply remove an app with a nine-year history from sale, ignore a developer's support requests for weeks, and provide no explanation of what's happening.

This kind of treatment is killing indie development.

Apple, please listen.

Thank you all for your support!
