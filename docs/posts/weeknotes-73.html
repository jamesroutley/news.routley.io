  • hey! hi! it's weeknotes time!
  • i have had One Big Headache™ for the last few days and a friend says that counts as a migraine because it doesn't go away with sleep, but i don't know if there's such a firm demarcation. either way, hurting brain feelings suck! this post is brought to you by coconut water and paracetamol.
  • this has been a Big Media Week in my world: i watched afronauts at the theatre; i watched you can see everything at the cinema and i have continued re-reading all about love by bell hooks.
  • the afronauts was gorgeous. we watched a preview version - and you could tell - but when they nail the staging it will be truly incredible to watch. i have issues with fictionalised accounts of things that have none of the gravitas they are given by the reimagining, to put it lightly. but i bought some cool merch and have new bones for a video essay i have been working on for fifty years about why we explore space instead of our oceans, as well the spread of internet access! we'll see if i eventually land the video essay plane on that one.
  • you can see everything was gorgeous too. it's also a thing i am unsure should exist. i get that nathan fielder is a trickster amongst comedians and documentarians and i was expecting more of a twist i guess? but i came out feeling nothing but more confused about elizabeth holmes the human, and bad for the victims of theranos who must be sick of all these reimaginings. thanks to watching it though, i have another piece of writing percolating about truth, observation, authenticity and being perceived online. maybe it will be a video.
  • all about love you'll have to wait for my full thoughts on even though it's a re-reading, but tldr i don't like this book lol.
  • been enjoying writing month so far! i am wondering if i could somehow keep up this pace for longer if i would eventually reset my baseline to be two thousand or more views a week (current!) instead of my usual two hundred or so? i know it's unfashionable on the indie web to want your writing to do something other than be: be art; be expression... i would quite like to write a book, and/or more edited articles, and views definitely increase the surface area of a potential lucky break right? also, if you could help with that reach out, i'd be super grateful!
  • do you know of any writers who write in public/"with the garage door up"? i think i will try and do more of this, and have mentioned it in the past on here, but since my internet connection is being annoying streaming it probably out for the foreseeable, as i can't guarantee a good connection. wondering how else i can do it without just publishing more words or content (which ends up detracting from the original point of sharing lol)?
  • i've been eating mango poppadoms, sour sweets, chips, so many chips, and burgers. i've been drinking every fizzy drink i can get my greedy hands on, copious amounts of builder's tea, and vanilla chai lattes.
  • i hope you've had a fantastic week and next week treats you so well. 💟
  • see you next time?
