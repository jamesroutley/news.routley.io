Mathober 2026 Prompts

Are you ready? October is right around the corner and you know what that means? Mathober! This years prompts are on the website mathober.com and below.

You can use any medium, do as many or few prompts as you wish, and whenever you wish – in other words – no rules. Except, <dramatic pause> maybe a guideline that I added as a note in the about page:

This is a human endeavor, to dive in, to contemplate, to struggle, to create, and to grow.

I look forward to seeing the creativity, discussion, and play. Enjoy!

This year I'd like to thank blinry for pairing on a fairly manual process to create the list and rolling the random numbers. From the random number, a word or phrase is pulled from a list in a spreadsheet. Some are thrown out and a number is re-rolled. The cut ones tend to be people's names, words with errors from an import issue, redundant from previous years, or overly simple. Sometimes, it might just be one word from a phrase to leave things open for interpretation.
